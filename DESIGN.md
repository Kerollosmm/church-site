## Brand & Style

This design system honors established ecclesiastical publishing norms with venerable liturgical maroon, dignified serif accents, and crisp institutional clarity. Built specifically for the Coptic Orthodox parish of Saints Maximus, Domadius, and the Great Martyr St. Moses the Black in Al-Asafra, Alexandria (under the See of St. Mark), the visual narrative reflects over seventeen centuries of living tradition balanced with contemporary pastoral accessibility.

The aesthetic philosophy draws directly from canonical church publications, parchment illuminated manuscripts, and the visual language of parish feast announcements:
- **Solemnity & Reverence:** Deep Imperial Burgundy `#5C101B` establishes liturgical authority, echoed by sacred gold accents `#C9933B` and `#D4AF37` derived from altar cross gilding and iconography halos.
- **Ecclesiastical Clarity:** Layouts emulate formal pastoral bulletins with clear hierarchical framing, fine dual-stroke gold rules, and crisp architectural arches.
- **RTL-First Scholarly Typographic Balance:** Dignified classical serifs pair seamlessly with authoritative Arabic scripts (Noto Serif with Noto Kufi Arabic headings; Noto Sans with Noto Sans Arabic body copy) to create an institutional, welcoming, and deeply devotional reading experience across announcements, multi-altar liturgical mass schedules, and humanitarian clinic rosters.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the sacred liturgical colors of the Coptic rite, balancing solemn deep reds, radiant sanctuary golds, and warm monastic parchment whites.

### Primary Palette (Liturgical Wine & Burgundy)
- **Liturgical Wine Anchor (`#3D0B12`):** Grounding foundation used for authoritative mastheads, institutional footers, and prominent ceremonial framing.
- **Imperial Liturgical Maroon (`#5C101B` — Primary Seed):** Used for primary pastoral actions, solemn calls-to-prayer, primary sanctuary badges (*الكنيسة فوق*), major title calligraphy, and parish seal medallions.
- **Active Wine (`#8A1C2C`):** Interactive hover and focus states on burgundy surfaces and active navigation tabs.

### Secondary & Accent Palette (Sacred Amber & Halo Gold)
- **Sacred Amber (`#C9933B`):** Warm, devotional amber utilized for glassmorphic schedule containers, secondary floor sanctuary tags (*الكنيسة تحت*), and section dividers.
- **Radiance Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Brilliant halo gold reserved for canonical cross glyphs, iconographic borders, and celebration highlights.
- **Sanctuary Warm Gold (`#E8A928`):** Architectural accent for bell tower notices, arch crowns, and urgent parish pastoral alerts.

### Neutral & Canvas Palette (Parchment & Charcoal)
- **Celestial Warm White (`#FDFBF7`):** The primary light canvas; evokes warm natural linen and soft interior stone of Alexandrian sanctuaries.
- **Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for cards, timetable matrices, and readable schedule inserts.
- **Aged Papyrus (`#F3E5AB`):** Ribbon badges, feast scripture verses, and memorial dedications.
- **Deep Obsidian Charcoal (`#1A1615` — Neutral):** Primary high-contrast ink color for maximum readability of Arabic and Latin body texts.
- **Muted Stone Text (`#4A3E3D`):** Secondary body copy, subtitle notes, and metadata labels.
- **Border Stroke Tint (`#E6DDD0`):** Soft, structural separator lines ensuring crispness without visual noise.

## Typography

The typography is optimized for Right-to-Left (RTL) reading hierarchy while accommodating Latin script, liturgical numbers, and tabular liturgy timings:
- **Headlines & Display:** Rendered using `Noto Serif` (or its canonical partner `Noto Kufi Arabic` for Arabic script titles) with weights ranging from Bold (700) to Extra-Bold (800). This captures the formal, authoritative gravitas of ecclesiastical stone carving and manuscript book titles.
- **Body & Continuous Scripture Reading:** Rendered using `Noto Sans` (and `Noto Sans Arabic`), set with generous line heights (`1.625rem` to `1.875rem`) to ensure effortless reading for elder parishioners reviewing lengthy gospel readings, fasting announcements, or synaxarium passages.
- **Timetable Figures & Reference Numbers:** Tabular lining figures are enforced across all liturgical service timings (Cairo Standard Time) and administrative registration identifiers (such as clinic queue codes) to prevent misalignment in parallel schedule tables.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column or 2-column stacked configuration on mobile devices.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas of `1280px`. Section margins are set to `2rem` (`32px`) and column gutters to `1.25rem` (`20px`). Complex multi-sanctuary liturgical schedules display side-by-side to allow simultaneous comparison between the Upper Altar (*المذبح الرئيسي*) and Lower Crypt Altar (*مذبح القديسين*).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Pastoral staff directories and clinic service matrices adjust to 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with compact outer margins `1rem` (`16px`) and `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters. Service timelines transition to vertical chronological steppers.

### Spacing Scale & Rhythm
Spacing intervals follow a steady 4px/8px modular rhythm. Card interiors and announcement containers enforce consistent vertical paddings:
- Small metadata badges and chips: `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
- Input groups and schedule row items: `space-md` (16px).
- Major container padding: `space-lg` (24px).
- Inter-section liturgical breathing room: `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve institutional clarity and avoid synthetic glossy trends, depth is expressed through subtle parchment layers, warm ambient halos, and crisp low-contrast golden borders.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Flat / Canvas (Elevation 0):** `#FDFBF7` Celestial Warm White canvas with subtle parchment undertones.
2. **Surface / Cards (Elevation 1):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces defined by a crisp `1px` border of `#E6DDD0` combined with a soft, warm ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(61, 11, 18, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Schedule & Liturgical Highlights (Elevation 2):** Semi-opaque gold/amber schedule cards utilize frosted ambient glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with background `rgba(201, 147, 59, 0.12)`, anchored by a `1px` hairline border in `#D4AF37`.
4. **Modals & Liturgical Feast Overlays (Elevation 3):** Deep liturgical maroon scrim (`rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.65)`) with cards elevated via deep dimensional drop shadows: `0 12px 32px rgba(61, 11, 18, 0.18)` and top-border gold highlight trims.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a conservative **Soft** shape geometry (`roundedness: 1`):
- Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, notification tags) use a compact `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius.
- Cards, schedule containers, and modals employ `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii (`rounded-lg`).
- **Architectural Coptic Arch Modifier:** Specialized clergy portraits, feast icons, and archival photographs utilize an arched crown (`rounded-t-full rounded-b-sm`), paying homage to the semicircular masonry archways characteristic of Alexandria's historic basilicas and sanctuaries.
- Circular Medallions: Parish seals, patriarchate emblems, and halo cross insignias are strictly circular (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Pastoral Button:** Background `#5C101B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #3D0B12`, corner radius `0.25rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to `#8A1C2C` with subtle gold ring focus indicator (`#D4AF37`).
- **Secondary Sanctuary Button:** Background transparent, text `#5C101B`, border `1px solid #C9933B`. On hover, fills with `rgba(201, 147, 59, 0.1)`.
- **Liturgical Gold Action:** Background `#C9933B`, text `#1A1615` (bold weight), border `1px solid #D4AF37`. Hover shifts to `#E8A928`.

### Multi-Sanctuary Floor Badges & Chips
- **Upper Main Altar Badge (*الكنيسة فوق*):** Solid `#5C101B` background, crisp `#FFFFFF` typography, `0.25rem` radius, accompanied by an orthodox cross icon prefix.
- **Lower Crypt Altar Badge (*الكنيسة تحت*):** Warm `#C9933B` background, deep charcoal `#1A1615` text, providing immediate structural discernment for parishioners navigating simultaneous dawn liturgies.
- **Status Indicator Badges:** Confession open, clinic booked, live stream broadcast active — set with a `0.25rem` radius, 12px font size, and `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding.

### Cards & Liturgical Announcement Containers
- **Multi-Sanctuary Mass Card:** Standard white card bordered by `#E6DDD0`. Features a dual-header displaying the target sanctuary badge, exact Cairo wall-clock timing in bold tabular figures, celebrant priest designation, and fasting requirements.
- **Liturgical Feast Hero Announcement:** Full-width deep burgundy `#3D0B12` to `#5C101B` gradient container, framed by a dual fine gold perimeter rule (`#D4AF37`). Flanked by dual circular medallions (Patriarchate seal on the right, Parish seal on the left). Hosts a central glassmorphic amber schedule container (`backdrop-blur: 8px`).
- **Clergy & Archival Cards:** Framed with the signature ecclesiastical arch (`rounded-t-full`), highlighted by a `2px` gold halo rim (`#D4AF37`), with a bottom aged papyrus ribbon (`#F3E5AB`) displaying ordained ordination dates and pastoral responsibilities.

### Form Inputs & Pastoral Service Fields (Zero-Auth Clinic & Services)
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#1A1615` text, `1px solid #E6DDD0` border, `0.25rem` radius. On active focus, border shifts to `#5C101B` with an ambient glow of `rgba(92, 16, 27, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with border `#5C101B`. When checked, solid `#5C101B` fill with `#FFFFFF` checkmark/indicator.
- **List Groups:** Alternating row highlights using `#FDFBF7` and `#FFFFFF`, separated by soft `#E6DDD0` rules. Timetable hours are right-aligned (for RTL flow) with prominent bold numbers.
